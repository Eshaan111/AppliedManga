# Applied-Task: experiment and local-model inventory

Scanned the project sources and C:/MODELS. This list records **71 experiment/test topics**; related topics can share one notebook or model. It is not a count of completed independent training runs. Backup copies under 1pageTest-EVERYTHING-GOOD and 1pageTest-EVERYTHING-ULTRA-GOOD are consolidated rather than counted again. Tutorial notebooks, vendored libraries and environment folders are not counted as model experiments.

Recorded outputs means saved notebook output exists, including possible errors or partial runs; it does not guarantee completion or accuracy. Implemented means a notebook exists but no saved execution was confirmed. Downloaded models without confirmed experiments are identified separately.

Updated 2026-10-16 with outcomes and lessons from saved artifacts and this conversation. **Observed** means directly supported by a saved result or documented user inspection. **Lesson/limitation** explains what the test establishes without claiming a measured gain. **Run unconfirmed** is not a failure verdict. Proposed causes are not presented as proven explanations. No new training, inference or development scoring was run for this update.

## Breadth at a glance

The work spans detection, OCR, layout/order, face/character identity, multimodal speaker attribution, decoder/encoder/merger adaptation, custom narration architectures, imbalance handling, hyperparameter search, contour geometry, transcript grouping and evaluation.

## Earlier approaches outside Magi

The project began with multiple alternatives: direct Qwen image-to-transcript generation and LoRA; separate YOLO bubbles, Paddle OCR, DASS face/body detections and InternVL association; manual/randomized-ID supervision; actual InternVL decision-weighted/full-assistant LoRA smoke runs; separate DINO identity; multipage preparation; Qwen attribution training; and independent layout/order models.

[Pre-Magi approaches: implementation, results and lessons](PRE_MAGI_APPROACHES.md) documents **15 approach families** with source links, including the actual saved training failures and the distinction between completed training and unconfirmed ablations. This reorganizes existing inventory topics rather than increasing the 71-topic count. Exact historical date ordering is not asserted.

## Scope distinctions useful when presenting this work

- We trained character embedding projections, final Magi crop-encoder blocks, narration heads/custom sequence classifiers, and decoder-adapter variants. We did not fine-tune every downloaded detector.
- Vision/projector LoRA ablation notebooks, the Manga109 matching-head training and some balanced/visual VL variants are implementations with unconfirmed execution; do not present all as completed training.
- MangaLMM is downloaded, but a dedicated experiment was not verified. Qwen3-4B/Qwen3-8B were researched as OCR-cleanup candidates; this inventory does not count that research as a downloaded/trained/tested model.
- The active contour narration labeler is heuristic. The project also contains independently learned narration models; both approaches were explored.
- The ByT5 test was not integrated after the user rejected its output. Dictionary/gibberish checks are diagnostic alternatives. The selected final pipeline uses Paddle-only Gemma artifact decisions; agreement/SFX deletion belongs to older pipelines.
